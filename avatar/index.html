Avatar Blind Test

Help us evaluate AI-generated talking head videos. You'll watch short clips and rate how realistic they look.

  1. You'll go through 5 rounds
  2. Rounds 1-4: Compare 5 different AI avatars saying the same thing with a specific emotion (casual, excited, reflective, playful)
  3. Round 5: Compare 6 avatars — some generated from a photo, others from training video
  4. Rate each clip on 4 dimensions using a 1-5 scale
  5. Clips are randomized — you won't know which system made which
  6. At the end, copy your results and send them to Andrew

Rating scale: 1 = Obviously AI, 5 = Could fool you into thinking it's real
